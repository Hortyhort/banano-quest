Privacy Policy

Banano Quest: Monkey Platformer — Last updated: February 2026

1. Overview

Banano Quest ("the Game") is a free, offline-capable platformer game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the Game collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect

Banano Quest collects no personal data. Specifically:

  • No account registration is required to play.
  • No names, emails, or identifiers are collected.
  • No location data is accessed.
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts permissions are requested.
  • No advertising SDKs are included.
  • No third-party analytics services transmit data off your device.

3. Local Storage

The Game stores gameplay data locally on your device using the browser's localStorage API. This data includes:

  • High scores and level progress
  • Settings preferences (sound, music, haptics)
  • Achievement unlock status
  • Gameplay statistics (coins collected, enemies stomped, play time)
  • Cosmetic skin selections
  • Anonymous gameplay event logs (for local analysis only)

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser data or uninstalling the application.

4. Children's Privacy

Banano Quest does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The Game contains no social features, chat, or user-generated content.

5. Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data. If distributed via the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, those platforms may collect their own usage data according to their respective privacy policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
