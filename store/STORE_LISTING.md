## Full Description

🍌 **Banano Quest: Monkey Platformer** 🍌

Guide your brave monkey through 5 action-packed worlds filled with coins, enemies, power-ups, and epic boss battles! Jump, stomp, and dash your way to victory in this classic platformer designed for mobile.

**🎮 FEATURES:**

⭐ **5 Unique Themed Worlds** — From sunny meadows to volcanic caverns, each level brings new challenges and visual delights.

⭐ **Classic Platformer Action** — Precise jump controls, enemy stomping, spike dodging, and moving platforms. Easy to learn, tough to master.

⭐ **Power-Up System** — Smash question blocks to discover speed boosts, double jumps, and coin magnets. Use them wisely!

⭐ **Epic Boss Fight** — Face off against the mighty boss at the end of Level 5. Three stomps to victory!

⭐ **12 Achievements** — From "First Steps" to "Speed Runner", unlock achievements that prove your skills.

⭐ **Unlockable Monkey Skins** — Earn golden, ice, lava, and shadow skins by completing challenges.

⭐ **Star Ratings** — Collect all coins in each level for a perfect 3-star rating. Can you get them all?

⭐ **Detailed Stats** — Track your lifetime coins, enemy stomps, play time, and best times per level.

⭐ **Works Offline** — Play anytime, anywhere. No internet required after install.

⭐ **No Ads, No In-App Purchases** — Pure gaming, no interruptions.

**🕹️ CONTROLS:**
• Touch: On-screen d-pad and jump button
• Desktop: Arrow keys / WASD + Space

**🎵 AUDIO:**
• Procedurally generated soundtrack and sound effects
• Haptic feedback on mobile devices
• Full audio controls in settings

Download now and start your Banano Quest adventure!
